Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. For emergency water extraction in Palo Alto, Palo Alto Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Ceilings, light fixtures or walls that are actively leaking water
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on
  • Keep valuables and stored boxes off the basement floor on shelves or pallets
  • Consider an automatic shutoff valve, which senses a leak and closes the water supply
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Program (947) 249-5149 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
